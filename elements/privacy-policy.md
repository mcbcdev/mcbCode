# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 21, 2026**

Welcome to mcbCode.

This Privacy Policy explains how mcbCode collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use mcbCode, including its website, editor, accounts, projects, Android application, and other related services (collectively, the "Service").

By using mcbCode, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

## 1. About mcbCode

mcbCode is operated by **idktoast** and is designed primarily for creating, editing, managing, and sharing content for Minecraft: Bedrock Edition.

mcbCode may provide features including:

- Code and file editing
- Project creation and management
- Project sharing
- Collaboration
- User profiles
- Documentation and educational resources
- File exporting
- GitHub synchronization
- AI-assisted tools when available
- Other tools and features introduced over time

mcbCode is an independent service and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Mojang Studios or Microsoft unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## 2. Information We Collect

mcbCode collects information that is necessary to provide accounts, projects, synchronization, and other features of the Service.

### Account Information

When you create or access an mcbCode account through a supported OAuth provider, mcbCode may receive and store information provided by that provider, which may include:

- User ID
- Username
- Email address
- Profile picture

The exact information provided may depend on the OAuth provider you use.

mcbCode currently supports authentication or account-related integrations with services including Google, GitHub, and Discord.

### Profile Information

Users may choose to provide or change information associated with their mcbCode profile, including:

- Username
- Biography
- Profile picture

### Project and User Content

mcbCode stores information and content associated with projects that users create or upload. This may include:

- Project names
- Project descriptions
- Project files and code
- Project visibility settings
- Collaboration information
- Snapshots or saved project versions
- Share codes or custom share links
- Other content submitted as part of a project

The information stored depends on the features you use.

### Device-Only Information

The mcbCode application may store application preferences and settings locally on your device.

These device-only settings are separate from your mcbCode account settings and are not treated as account information stored by mcbCode's servers.

## 3. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

mcbCode does not intentionally store the following information on its own servers as part of normal Service operation:

- IP addresses
- Device model information
- Android version or other operating system version information
- Browser type
- Precise location
- Approximate location
- Advertising identifiers
- General device telemetry or usage analytics

However, third-party services used by the Service, including advertising providers, may collect or process information independently of mcbCode. Their collection and processing are governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

## 4. How We Use Information

mcbCode may use collected information to:

- Create and maintain user accounts
- Authenticate users
- Display and manage user profiles
- Store and synchronize projects and files
- Provide collaboration features
- Provide project sharing features
- Provide GitHub synchronization when enabled by the user
- Provide security and prevent unauthorized account access
- Operate, maintain, and improve the Service
- Respond to support or privacy requests
- Comply with applicable legal requirements

mcbCode does not currently use account information for third-party behavioral advertising.

## 5. Cookies and Local Storage

mcbCode uses cookies and browser storage technologies such as localStorage where necessary to operate the Service.

These technologies may be used for purposes including:

- Keeping users signed in
- Maintaining sessions
- Remembering preferences
- Supporting authentication and security
- Providing website functionality

mcbCode does not use its own cookies or localStorage data to intentionally provide third parties with personal information for unrelated tracking purposes.

Some third-party services may use their own technologies when their services are used through mcbCode.

## 6. Third-Party Services

mcbCode relies on third-party services to operate certain parts of the Service. These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective functionality.

mcbCode currently uses or may use services provided by:

- **Cloudflare** for infrastructure and hosting-related services
- **Google** for account authentication and, for the Android application, advertising services through Google AdMob
- **GitHub** for account authentication and optional GitHub synchronization
- **Discord** for account authentication
- **Monetag** for advertising on parts of the mcbCode website

Third-party services are controlled by their respective providers. Their processing of information may be subject to their own privacy policies, terms, and data practices.

## 7. Google AdMob and Advertising

The mcbCode Android application may display advertisements through **Google AdMob**.

Because AdMob is a third-party advertising service, Google may process certain information through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and related advertising systems. Depending on the configuration of the Service, applicable law, and a user's settings, this may include information such as app version, device category, device model, operating system information, country, advertising-related identifiers, and information used for advertising measurement or personalization.

mcbCode does not directly store this advertising information as part of its own account database unless otherwise stated in this Privacy Policy.

Advertising practices may change as the application and its advertising configuration change. Google provides additional information about its advertising and privacy practices through its own policies.

## 8. Monetag and Website Advertising

Some parts of the mcbCode website may display advertisements provided by Monetag.

Monetag may process information according to its own privacy policy and advertising technologies. mcbCode does not control the information that Monetag independently collects or processes through its advertising systems.

The Android application and website may use different advertising providers.

## 9. GitHub Synchronization

mcbCode may allow users to connect their GitHub account and synchronize selected repositories with mcbCode.

GitHub synchronization requires the user's permission before it is enabled.

mcbCode requests repository edit access only to repositories that the user allows through the GitHub authorization process.

GitHub access credentials or tokens used for synchronization are encrypted when stored by mcbCode.

mcbCode does not request unrestricted access to all repositories when the GitHub integration is configured for selected repositories.

## 10. Account Authentication and Security

mcbCode uses OAuth-based authentication through supported providers rather than requiring users to provide a password directly to mcbCode for those authentication methods.

mcbCode may store provider identifiers and account information necessary to recognize and authenticate an account.

The mcbCode application may also use temporary security codes as part of the device synchronization process. These codes help ensure that a person who has physical access to a device cannot simply sign into another person's account without authorization.

mcbCode uses encrypted connections, access controls, and other reasonable technical measures intended to protect information while it is transmitted and stored.

No online service can guarantee complete security, and users should take reasonable steps to protect their own accounts and devices.

## 11. AI Features

mcbCode may provide AI-assisted features in the future.

At the time of this Privacy Policy, the mcbCode AI assistant known as DripstoneAI is not currently operating as part of the Service.

When AI features become available again, this Privacy Policy may be updated to explain what information is sent to external AI providers and how that information is handled.

## 12. How We Share Information

mcbCode may share information with service providers when sharing is reasonably necessary to provide or operate a feature of the Service.

Examples include:

- Cloudflare for infrastructure and hosting-related services
- Google, GitHub, or Discord when required for authentication or account integrations
- GitHub when a user explicitly enables GitHub synchronization
- Google AdMob for advertising in the Android application
- Monetag for advertising on applicable parts of the website

mcbCode does not sell user account information as a standalone product.

mcbCode does not intentionally share private project content with unrelated third parties except where necessary to provide a feature selected by the user, operate the Service, protect the Service, or comply with applicable law.

When you choose to make content public or share it with another person, that content may be accessible to people other than you and may be copied, downloaded, or redistributed by those people.

## 14. Data Retention

mcbCode generally retains account and project information for as long as the associated account remains active, unless you request otherwise or a feature requires a different retention period.

mcbCode may retain information for the period reasonably necessary to provide the requested Service and to respond to user requests.

Temporary information, such as information used to support device synchronization, may be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for that feature.

When account deletion is completed, mcbCode removes the data tied to the deleted account as described in the Account Deletion section below.

## 15. Account Deletion and Data Removal

Users may request deletion of their mcbCode account and associated data.

To delete an account, visit:

https://mcbcode.com/dashboard

After signing in, scroll down to the **Delete Account** section and follow the provided steps.

mcbCode is designed to immediately wipe data tied to the deleted account, including account information and associated project data.

Because third-party services operate independently, deletion of information held directly by those providers may also be subject to their own systems, policies, or deletion processes.
