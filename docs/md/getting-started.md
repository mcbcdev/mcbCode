# Getting Started with the mcbCode Editor

Welcome to mcbCode! This guide will help you get familiar with the editor workspace so you can start writing, saving, and exporting your Minecraft Bedrock `.mcfunction` scripts with ease.


## Editor Layout Tour

The editor is broken down into four easy-to-use areas:

1. **Top Bar:** This is where you name your file, access your account, and save your progress.
2. **Menu Bar (File, Edit, View, Help):** Located just below the top bar. Click these to open dropdown menus for exporting code, changing editor themes, or looking up keyboard shortcuts.
3. **Left Sidebar:** Keeps track of your current font size and shows you if your computer's modifier keys (Ctrl, Shift, Alt) are actively being pressed.
4. **Code Canvas:** The large dark area where you type your commands. 


## Managing Your Files

### Naming Your File
At the top of the screen, click inside the box next to **Filename:** to type a custom name for your script. The box automatically stretches to fit your text. You don't need to type `.mcfunction` at the end—the editor adds that for you automatically when you export.

### Exporting and Copying Your Work
Click on the **File** menu in the top left to choose how you want to extract your code:
* **Copy Code:** Instantly copies your entire script to your computer's clipboard so you can paste it anywhere.
* **Export as .mcfunction:** Downloads the file directly to your computer, ready to be dropped into a Minecraft behavior pack. Any invalid characters in your filename will automatically turn into underscores (`_`) to keep the file safe for Minecraft.
* **Export as .txt:** Downloads your code as a standard text document.


## Saving Your Work to the Cloud

To keep your scripts safe and sync them across different devices, you can link your code to a free mcbCode account.

1. Click [here](https://mcbcode.com/account) to log in.
2. Once you are signed in, a **Save** button will appear in the top bar of the editor.
3. Click **Save** to back up your file to the cloud. The editor will change the web address in your browser (`?save=YOUR_ID`) so you can bookmark it or share your code with a friend.<br>
*Don't worry, only you can edit this file. But anyone can view it with the correct link.*
4. Watch the button text for live status updates: `Saving...` means it is uploading, `Saved!` means your work is secure, and `Error` means something went wrong with the connection.
