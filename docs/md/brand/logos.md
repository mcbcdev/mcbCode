# logos

the mcbcode logo is available in several variants for different backgrounds, states, and uses.

when using the logo, keep its proportions intact and do not stretch, rotate, or modify the artwork.

## logo usage

the mcbcode logo should remain visually consistent wherever it is used.

please do not:

* stretch or squash the logo
* rotate the logo
* alter its proportions
* add effects or filters
* recolor variants without a specific reason
* remove parts of the logo artwork

use the variant that best matches the surrounding interface or background.
