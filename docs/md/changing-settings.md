# Editing Account Settings

Account settings contain the preferences and configuration options for your mcbCode profile. Modifying these settings allows you to personalize your mcbCode user experience across the workspace and platform.

Most changes made in your account settings are saved automatically upon selection unless an explicit **Save** button is provided. While many adjustments take effect immediately across the editor or website, certain changes may require a browser page refresh to take full effect.

## Opening Account Settings

To access and modify your account settings, follow these steps:

1. Sign in to your mcbCode account.
2. Open the **Profile** page at [mcbcode.com/profile](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://mcbcode.com/profile).
3. Select the **Settings** button below your username.
4. Scroll to the specific setting you wish to modify.
5. Update the setting using the provided controls (e.g., toggles, dropdown menus, or text fields).

## Overview of Settings

Account settings are grouped into functional sections within the settings menu.

| Scope | Description | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Global Site Settings** | Preferences that apply across the entire mcbCode platform. | Interface preferences, communication options. |
| **Editor Settings** | Options that modify your workspace and coding interface. | Display behavior, editor tool configurations. |
| **Privacy & Security** | Controls for account interaction and profile visibility. | Collaboration requests, visible profile details. |

> **Note:** For specific instructions on managing collaboration requests, consult the dedicated **Managing Collaboration Requests** documentation page.

## Notes

* **Account Scope:** Setting changes apply strictly to the currently signed-in account and do not affect other users or guest sessions.
* **Impact Area:** Some settings exclusively affect the editor workspace, while others alter behavior across the entire mcbCode website.
* **Visibility:** Certain profile information and social settings may be visible to other mcbCode users depending on your configured privacy settings.

## Troubleshooting

### A Setting Does Not Appear to Change

* Ensure your internet connection is active so saved preferences can sync with mcbCode servers.
* If a setting control includes an explicit **Save** or **Apply** button, confirm that you clicked it after making changes.

### Changes Are Not Visible After Updating

* Refresh your browser page to ensure the application reloads with your newly updated configuration.
* If the setting applies specifically to the editor, close and reopen your active project window.

### A Setting Appears Unavailable or Grayed Out

* Confirm that you are properly signed in with an active account.
* Certain settings may require specific account conditions or prerequisites to be met before they can be edited.
