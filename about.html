About mcbCode

mcbCode was created to provide an editor built specifically for Minecraft Bedrock Edition development. Rather than adapting a general-purpose code editor, every feature is designed around the workflow of creating behavior packs, resource packs, and add-ons.

The platform continues to expand with collaboration features, project management, integrated development tools, and services that help creators spend less time managing files and more time building.

About the Creator

idktoast

idktoast is the creator of mcbCode and an active Minecraft Bedrock developer. What began as a one page function editor gradually evolved into a platform dedicated to improving the Bedrock development experience for creators of every skill level.
